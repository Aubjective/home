Discover Padvia

Sound, shaped your way.

Play with confidence

Choose major or minor chords in all 12 keys. Hold a key for one second to sustain it while playing other chords. Blend up to three layers, set their levels and fades, and expand Live keys for bigger touch targets, including landscape.

Find your sound

Explore 20 sound families and 16 built-in presets, from soft piano and voices to bowed textures, organ, glassy tones and nature ambience. Save custom mixes and favorite sounds.

Prepare your set

Build setlists with each song's key, mix and chord sequence. Advance with a large Next chord button and use sharp or flat naming based on the song key.

Motion & notes

Optionally add subtle movement and spacious notes around a sustained chord, with Soft glow, Felt piano or Warm mallets. These controls do not automatically change chord progression.

Shape the whole mix

Fine-tune tone, EQ, reverb, echo and fades. Save layers, levels and effects as a preset or as part of a song. In-app help includes practical examples.

Offline sounds · Two languages

Prepared built-in sounds can play offline. Interfaces are available in English and Simplified Chinese. Rewarded videos, purchases and restoring purchases require internet access.

Start free, expand when you need more

Get started with 4 free sounds and 2 built-in presets. Both free presets use only free sounds.

For full access, watch 2 rewarded videos to unlock all sounds, presets and features for 24 hours, or make one non-consumable purchase for permanent full access. There is no subscription.

The 24-hour unlock covers the whole app, not just one sound or feature. Rewarded-ad availability may vary.
